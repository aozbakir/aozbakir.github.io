**Highlights:**

* 123 residential energy studies published from 2014 to 2025 are reviewed.
* Evaluation shifts from model accuracy to household interventions.
* Only 13 studies primarily test interventions at real residential sites.
* Urban scaling requires transfer, grid, equity, and governance evidence.
