Ongoing course covering classical and modern time-series forecasting methods, structured as three theory lectures plus a hands-on lab. Lecture 1 covers loading and visualizing real-world time series (using an Arctic climate dataset) and decomposing them into trend, seasonal, and residual components. Lecture 2 covers stationarity (ADF testing), transformations (differencing, log, Box-Cox), autocorrelation (ACF/PACF), and baseline forecasting. Lecture 3 covers Auto-ARIMA, Prophet, and residual diagnostics, comparing models by MAPE. The lab session has students build a complete end-to-end forecasting pipeline — from raw data to a tuned ARIMA model with forecast intervals — on a new dataset.
