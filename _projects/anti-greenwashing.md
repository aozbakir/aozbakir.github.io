A private collaboration between Open Universiteit and APG on AI-based detection of corporate greenwashing. Grounded in a systematic literature review (PRISMA methodology) covering how greenwashing is defined, what datasets and AI techniques existing solutions use (transformer-based classifiers such as BERT, RoBERTa, and ClimateBERT), how they're evaluated, and what technical, governance, and ethical recommendations follow.

With Deniz Iren, Clara Maathuis, Gerard Mertens, Karen Maas, and Petru Curseu at Open Universiteit.

**Technology:** Python pipeline (Hugging Face transformers, spaCy, Azure Document Intelligence for PDF paragraph extraction) computing a Cheap Talk Index (per Bingler et al., 2024) from corporate sustainability disclosures, tested on real annual reports (ASML, Audi, Heineken, H&M).
