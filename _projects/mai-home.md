An Interreg Flanders–Netherlands project (started September 2023) combating energy poverty and reducing CO₂ emissions from housing across Dutch and Flemish Limburg, North Brabant, and Antwerp. Combines sensor-based monitoring of living behavior with a self-learning algorithm that gives residents personalized energy-saving guidance through gamification and social learning, on the premise that renovation alone doesn't sustain energy savings once residents' motivation fades. Also runs educational workshops and a MOOC for housing-corporation and municipal staff. Coordinated by Open Universiteit with five housing corporations, SMEs, and social/knowledge institutions.

Has produced peer-reviewed papers in *Energy and Buildings* and at ACM SAC, and the HOMESENSE multisensor dataset (accepted, *Scientific Data*) — see [Publications](/publications/).
