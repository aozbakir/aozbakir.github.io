I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science / AI at [Open Universiteit](https://www.ou.nl/), Heerlen, Netherlands, where I teach Time Series Analysis and Forecasting and co-developed the MSc course Software Engineering and AI.

My research centers on machine learning for time series — occupancy inference and energy optimization from sparse IoT sensor data (RNNs, Transformers, graph neural networks) — and on multi-agent systems for peer-to-peer energy trading, including the design of blockchain-based smart contracts for battery storage markets. I also work on the societal effects of AI, including disinformation, epistemic trust, and AI ethics in education.

Before moving into computer science, I trained as a geophysicist (PhD, Utrecht University), working on GNSS network analysis, earthquake hazard modeling, and Earth observation. That background still shapes how I think about probabilistic modeling and messy, real-world sensor data.

I currently supervise PhD candidate Yu Sheng on AI-driven energy optimization as part of the MAI-HOME project.

A full list of positions and publications is on my [CV](/cv/) and [Publications](/publications/) pages.
